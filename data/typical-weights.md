# Typical weights — for estimating what has not been weighed

Use these only to fill `weight_source: estimated` entries. They are ranges, not figures, and
the spread within a row is often wide enough to matter across a whole bag — which is why
`/travel-packing:weigh` reconciles the itemised total against the actual scale reading rather
than trusting a sum of estimates.

**Weigh the heavy end.** Mass in luggage is concentrated: shoes, outerwear, electronics,
toiletries and books usually account for most of a bag. Ten minutes with a kitchen scale on
those categories removes most of the error; estimating socks changes nothing.

All figures in grams, per item unless stated.

## Footwear

| Item | Range (pair) |

| Trainers / running shoes | 600–900 |
| Leather shoes | 800–1200 |

Shoes are the highest-leverage category in most bags: heaviest pair worn, third pair dropped.

leaving behind, and the first thing worth decanting.

## Containers

Belongs in `bags.yaml` as tare, never in the inventory.

| Item | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Cabin case, hard, 55 cm | 2500–3800 |
| Cabin case, soft, 55 cm | 1800–2800 |
| Checked case, medium hard | 3200–4500 |
| Checked case, large hard | 4000–5500 |
| Checked case, large soft | 3000–4200 |
| Duffel, large | 800–1500 |
| Backpack, 30 L | 700–1300 |
| Packing cube, medium | 80–140 |
| Compression cube | 120–200 |

A large hard case can spend a fifth of a 23 kg allowance on itself. When the allowance is
tight and the trip is not, the cheapest kilograms available are in the choice of case.
