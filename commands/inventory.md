# Inventory

Build or update `inventory.yaml` — what is being carried, by weight.

Invoke the `packing-inventory` skill and follow it. Offer the capture modes rather than forcing
one: dictation, photograph, category sweep, or import from a previous trip workspace.

Finish by reporting the item count, the itemised total, the split by weight source, and the ten
heaviest items — that last list is where all the headroom comes from.

`$ARGUMENTS` may name a bag to inventory, or a previous trip workspace to import from.
