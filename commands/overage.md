# Overage

Price every way out of being over the allowance and recommend one.

Invoke the `overage-options` skill and follow it. Check redistribution before pricing anything —
under a piece concept an overweight bag beside a light one costs money for nothing.

Write `overage.md`, make a single recommendation with its runner-up, and state the decision
deadline as an absolute date and time. Online bag purchase closes; that cutoff is the only
part of this that expires.
