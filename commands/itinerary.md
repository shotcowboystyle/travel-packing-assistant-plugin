# Itinerary

Capture or amend the flights for this trip into `itinerary.yaml`.

Invoke the `itinerary-intake` skill and follow it. Ask for the booking confirmation before
asking questions — the document carries the fare brand, the operating carrier and the ticket
grouping, which are the fields that decide the allowance and the fields nobody remembers.

`$ARGUMENTS` may contain a pasted itinerary, a path to a confirmation file, or nothing.
