## Directives for agents working here

**The numbers in this workspace are load-bearing.** Someone stands at a check-in desk with
them. Two rules follow:

1. **Never invent an allowance.** Every figure in `allowances.yaml` carries a source URL, the
   sentence it came from, and a confidence level. If you could not read it on the carrier's own
   page, its confidence is not `confirmed`, and it must stay visibly flagged everywhere it
   appears — including in the rendered PDFs.

2. **Measured beats itemised.** Where a bag has both a scale reading and a sum of item weights,
   the scale reading is the planning number and the difference is carried as unaccounted mass.
   Do not reconcile a gap by adjusting item estimates until they agree.

Weights: **grams for items** (`weight_g`), **kilograms for bags and allowances** (`_kg`).

`plan.yaml` is generated by `scripts/pack_solver.py`. Do not hand-edit it — regenerate.

Scale readings **append**. Never overwrite a previous weighing; the history across legs is
the data.

## After the trip

Record the final check-in weights (`phase: final`) and what actually happened at the desk —
whether the cabin bag was weighed, whether the allowance matched the research, what the
overage really cost. That is the only way the next trip's estimates get better, and it takes
two minutes while it is still true.
